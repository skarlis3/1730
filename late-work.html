How to take a late extension

This applies to the Literary Criticism Paper and the Final Project Check-in. You do not need to explain why, and there is no penalty. You do need to tell me.

  1. In Canvas, go to Grades.
  2. Click the assignment name.
  3. Add a comment saying you need the extension.

That's it. You then have seven days from the original due date to submit.

The seven days are firm

After seven days past the due date, the assignment can no longer receive credit. If something serious is going on, talk to me before you hit that point rather than after.

In-class work

Both of these happen during class and are graded pass/fail. Neither can be made up — the dropped grades exist so that missing one class does not cost you.

In-class freewrites

  • Completed during class.
  • You need to have done the reading to complete the freewrite.
  • Graded pass/fail: did you complete it, and does it show you read the text.
  • Cannot be made up.
  • The lowest two are dropped — that covers an absence, a late arrival, or one class where you did not finish the reading.

In-class participation

  • Completed during class.
  • Graded pass/fail on taking part in active learning activities and class discussions.
  • Cannot be made up.
  • The lowest two are dropped.

  • Exams cannot be retaken.
