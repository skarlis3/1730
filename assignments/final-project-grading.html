Grading

Reminder

Double check the Checklist page to ensure you didn’t miss or forget any requirements.

Your Final Project is graded holistically. The grade reflects how well the project works as a whole, using the descriptions below.

A (95%)

Your three reviews meet the requirements. The points you list for each review are specific claims about the text. Each point is connected to moments you quote from the text, and each quote has an explanation of whether it supports or pushes back against the point and why. Your explanations show that you have thought carefully about the text. Your display meets all of the presentation requirements. It is freestanding, polished, easy to follow, and fits the reading, and it includes the original reviews.

Note: an A is 95%, but exceptionally done presentations may earn up to 100%

B (85%)

Your three reviews meet the requirements. Most of your points are specific claims, and most of your quotes have explanations, but some explanations repeat the quote or the point instead of explaining why. Your display meets the presentation requirements, but it is less polished or harder to follow in places.

C (75%)

Your reviews mostly meet the requirements. Several of your points are general rather than specific, or some quotes connect only loosely to the points they are placed with. Some explanations are missing or very brief. Your display is missing one or more of the presentation requirements, or it is hard to follow in places.

D (65%)

One or more of your reviews doesn’t meet the requirements. Many of your points are general, and few of your quotes are explained. Your display is disorganized, is not freestanding, or is missing most of the original reviews.

F (50% or lower)

The project is missing major parts, such as one or more of the reviews or the connections between the reviews and the text.
