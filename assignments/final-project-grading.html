Grading

Double check the Checklist page to ensure you didn’t miss or forget any requirements.

Your Final Project is graded holistically. The grade reflects how well the project works as a whole, using the descriptions below.

A (95%)

Your project is thoughtful, well developed, and well designed. Each part of the project meets the requirements. Your points are specific, your explanations show careful thinking about the text, and your take is thoughtful and connected to the text. Your display is polished, easy to follow, and fits the reading.

Note: an A is 95%, but exceptionally done presentations may earn up to 100%

B (85%)

Your project meets the requirements and is strong overall, but it falls short of an A in one or more of these areas:

  • Some points or explanations are general.
  • Your take leans on summary or connects only loosely to the text.
  • Your display is less polished in places.

C (75%)

Your project meets most of the requirements, but it has problems in one or more of these areas:

  • Your reviews don’t fully meet the requirements.
  • Many points or explanations are general, brief, or missing.
  • Your take is short or doesn’t connect to the text.
  • Your display is missing requirements or is hard to follow.

D (65%)

Your project has serious problems in one or more of these areas:

  • Your reviews don’t meet the requirements.
  • Most points are general, and few quotes are explained.
  • Your take is missing or far too short.
  • Your display is disorganized or not freestanding.

F (50% or lower)

The project is missing major parts, such as one or more of the reviews, the connections between the reviews and the text, or Your Take.
