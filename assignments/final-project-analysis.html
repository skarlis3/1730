Final Project

The Analysis

The analysis is where you connect the reviews to the text itself. You likely will want to start with the specific points you listed for your Check-in, but you are not limited to those if you change your mind as you work on it. For each point, go back to your chosen text and find moments that either support the point or push back against it.

For each review:

  • List 3–5 specific points the reviewer makes about the text.
  • For each point, find quotes from the text that support it or push back against it.
  • For each quote, write a small blurb explaining whether it supports the point or pushes back against it, and why.

Explaining Your Quotes

Saying that a quote supports or pushes back against a point isn’t enough on its own. Your blurb needs to explain what in the quote connects to the point.

Explaining Your Quotes — Examples

Point: “She thought the ending was disappointing because the reader never sees the character change and take responsibility.”

Too general: “This quote supports her point.”

Specific: “This quote supports her point because in the last scene he still blames his brother for what happened, so he hasn’t taken responsibility by the end.”

Summary or Your Own Review

Choose one of the following:

  • Summarize the reviews and add your take on the reviews as a whole.
    • Such as… look at where the reviewers agree, where they split, and what the professional and non-professional reviews noticed differently.
  • Write your own review of the reading.
