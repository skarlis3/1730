Assignments

In-class freewrites

How they work

  • Write for the time assigned — typically 7 minutes — straight through.
  • Do not stop writing. Just write whatever comes to mind for the whole time period.
  • It doesn't need to be polished or well organized. Stream of consciousness is fine.
  • These will be about the texts we're discussing that day.
  • You must do the reading to complete the freewrite.

Do not try to make something up. Write that you haven't completed the reading, and then write about whatever you want related to literature, the class, and so on. That earns low partial credit for the day.

To receive credit

  • Be in class.
  • Be on time.
  • Have completed the reading.

Two are dropped

The lowest two freewrites are dropped, to account for an absence, being late, or not completing a reading.
