Final Project Check-in

The Check-in assignment is to help keep you on track and allow me to see your progress. For the check-in, please include the following:

  • The reading you chose (i.e. the novel or short story)
  • The reviews you are planning to use, including:
    • The title of the review
    • Author of review (for social media, the user name is fine)
    • A working link to the review
    • Whether this is a professional or non-professional review
    • Note: You need a minimum of three reviews for the check-in; you can include more than three if you are still deciding on which reviews to use.
  • For each review, include a few bullet points that summarize the specific points the author/content creator makes about the text.
    • These points should be more specific than “It was good”. See the examples below for general vs. specific claims.
    • To check a point, ask whether you could find a section of your chosen text (i.e. the novel or short story you chose) to quote that will either support it or push against it. If you couldn’t, the point is too general.

Specific Points from the Reviews — Examples

Too general: “She thought the ending was disappointing.”

Specific: “She thought the ending was disappointing because the reader never sees the character change and take responsibility.”

Note

Your choice of the reading and the reviews should not drastically change after your check-in. If you are concerned about your choices, you must reach out to me first to discuss. (In other words, your Final Project should seem like a natural progression from your check-in, not a brand new project.)
