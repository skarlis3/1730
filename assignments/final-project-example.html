Final Project

Final Project: Example Layouts

Your presentation needs to be freestanding and well organized. You aren’t required to use a tri-fold poster if you have another idea for how to display your work. You could build something or put something else together, as long as it looks polished and/or themed to the text.

Your Three Reviews

You need one professional review and one non-professional review. Your third review can be your choice, but it has to be from a different publication than your other two. Do not use sites that are meant to be study guides, such as SparkNotes or Studocu.

Types of reviews

  • Professional reviews: newspapers, magazines, and online professional publications, such as The New York Times, The New Yorker, Harvard Review, NPR, and Vulture
  • Non-professional reviews: social media, Goodreads, blogs, and similar sources, such as Reddit, TikTok, YouTube videos, personal blogs, Substack, and Medium articles

Example sets

  • A TikTok review, a Goodreads review, and a New York Times review
  • A Reddit review, a blog review, and a Harvard Review review
  • A YouTube video review, a Substack review, and a New Yorker review
