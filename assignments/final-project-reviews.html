Finding and Choosing Reviews

You will then need to find three reviews of the reading you chose to analyze. You will likely need to read/watch a number of reviews to find ones that work well for this project.

Required reviews:

  • One professional review
  • One non-professional review
  • One review of your choice

Professional reviews: newspapers, magazines, and online professional publications, such as The New York Times, The New Yorker, Harvard Review, NPR, and Vulture.

Non-professional reviews: social media, Goodreads, blogs, and similar sources, such as Reddit, TikTok, YouTube videos, personal blogs, Substack, and Medium articles.

Review of your choice: The third review can be either another non-professional review or another professional review. It just has to be from a different site or publication than the first two choices. In other words, if you chose a New York Times review and a Goodreads review, the third review cannot also be Goodreads.

Note

Do not use sites that are meant to be study guides, such as SparkNotes or Studocu, or AI summaries. These are not reviews.

Some examples of combinations:

  • A TikTok review, a Goodreads review, and a New York Times review
  • A blog review, a Harvard Review review, and a Reactor online magazine review
  • A Reddit review, a blog review, and a review from the Ancillary Review of Books
  • A YouTube video review, a Substack review, and a New Yorker review

The above are just examples. As long as you have three reviews, including one professional and one non-professional, you can choose from any sites or publications that work for your project.

Tips for Finding Reviews

Finding the right text

Some titles with common words in them (like Feed) may need the author’s name.

Instead of: Feed review
Try: Feed M. T. Anderson review

Finding the type of publication

Some titles are heavily reviewed, and you are likely to find reviews from a quick Google search. For others, you may need to be more specific.

If searching for the title and review isn’t working, try one or more of the following:
[title of reading] review [name of publication or site]

For example:
The House in the Cerulean Sea review New York Times
