Final Project

Your Take

After you have completed your analysis, you will write up/create your own take. For this part, you will have two options.

Choose ONE of the following options:

  • Write your take on the reviews
  • Write your own review

Your take on the reviews

For this option, you will summarize the reviews and then add your take on the reviews as a whole. Look at things like where the reviewers agree, where they split, and what the professional and non-professional reviews noticed differently.

Your own review

For this option, you will write your own review of the text. This can be a professional style review or a blog review. The review should include your opinions, and connect to specific about parts of the novel or short story.

For either option, this part of the project should be a minimum of 400 words.
