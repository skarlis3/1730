Emergencies and Significant Extenuating Circumstances

If you have an actual emergency or significant extenuating circumstance, please let me know as soon as possible. Depending on your progress in the class, an “Incomplete” may be available to you in order to complete the class after the semester is complete. Documentation of the emergency or circumstance may be required in order to receive an incomplete.

Incomplete Grade Policy for ENGL 1730

In this class, incomplete grades are reserved for students who are doing well in the class but are unable to submit the end of semester work due to significant, unexpected circumstances. An Incomplete will only be considered if 1) the student is passing the class with a C or above, 2) all other major assignments have been completed, and 3) the final project and/or final exam is the only remaining work.
