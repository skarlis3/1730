Final Project

Overview

Your Final Project will ask you to analyze how one of the readings this semester has been received and interpreted by looking at several different reviews on the text you choose. You will then create a presentation to display during the Final Project Presentation Day class (last day of class). Think science fair not speech style of presentation.

There will be two parts to this assignment:

  • Final Project Check-in: 5% of your grade
  • Final Project: 30% of your grade

Process Steps

  1. Choose your reading
  2. Find reviews
  3. Complete your Final Project Check-in assignment
  4. Do the analysis and connection work between the reviews and the text
  5. Plan out and create your display
