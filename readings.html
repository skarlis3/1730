How to get hold of things

Five books come from the bookstore. Everything else is free — either already online, or through the library, or posted for you.

Each entry below says how to reach it under How to read it. Where a text lives on the open web, the original link is there too: read it wherever you like, but the copy posted for class is the one that's been checked for screen readers and reflows properly on a phone.

The reading list

Five of the semester's texts. Every one gets the same fields, in the same order, whatever it is and wherever it came from.

  1. 01

    Friday Black

    Short stories

    Nana Kwame Adjei-Brenyah

    Year
    2018
    Comes to you via
    Mariner Books — trade publication, reviews, course adoption
    Gatekeeper
    An acquiring editor
    How to read it
    • Purchased text — buy it at the bookstore

    Twelve speculative, satirical stories about race and consumption in America — among them a theme park where paying customers act out racial violence, and a Black Friday sale that not every shopper survives.

  2. 02

    The Song of Achilles

    Novel · retelling

    Madeline Miller

    Year
    2012
    Comes to you via
    Ecco — Orange Prize, then a decade later, BookTok
    Gatekeeper
    A prize jury, then an algorithm
    How to read it
    • Purchased text — buy it at the bookstore

    The Iliad told from Patroclus's point of view — an exiled prince's lifelong love for Achilles, from their boyhood together to the war that kills them both.

  3. 03

    Gatekeeper

  5. 05

    Fun Home

    Graphic memoir

    Alison Bechdel

    Year
    2006
    Comes to you via
    Mariner — prizes, a Tony-winning musical, repeated challenges
    Gatekeeper
    A school board
    How to read it
    • Purchased text — buy it at the bookstore

    Bechdel's memoir about her closeted father — a small-town funeral director and English teacher — who died weeks after she came out in college, told through the books they both read.

Before you read the fanfiction

Reading a fic for class is not the same as being in that fic's community. The author is a private person who wrote something for free and did not sign up to be coursework. Don't comment on the fic as a student, don't tag the author, and don't bring the classroom into the archive.
