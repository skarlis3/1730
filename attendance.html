Policies

Attendance

Since this is an in-person, discussion-based course, attendance is essential. The core work of the class—small-group discussions, breakout activities, and full-class conversations—happens live and is not recorded or available online.

Participation & Freewrites: Your grade includes participation and the in-class freewrite (completed during the first 5–10 minutes of class). To account for illness or emergencies, the two lowest freewrite/participation scores are automatically dropped.

Arrive On-time: Because the freewrite happens at the very start of class, arriving late means missing the assignment.

What happens if I miss a class?

This course is discussion-based and student-led. Each class follows the same structure: freewrite, small-group discussion, class activity, and full-class discussion. Because the substance of each class is generated by the students who attend, there are no lecture notes, slides, or summaries available for missed class meetings — there is no way to condense three hours of interactive discussion into an email or document.

If you miss a class:

  • Check the Readings page on Canvas for what's assigned next. Unless otherwise announced, readings and any upcoming assignments listed there are the only homework — there is no additional homework assigned in class.
  • Freewrites cannot be made up. Your two lowest freewrite scores are dropped to account for this, so missing one does not affect your grade.
  • Class discussions will deepen your understanding of the readings, but the exams are not designed to punish you for missing a class. Essay questions on the midterm and final are based on the readings, not from a transcript of class discussion. In other words, if you've actively read a text, you can write about it. That said, missing more than one class in an exam period makes this harder.
